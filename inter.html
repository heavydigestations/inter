Redundant flesh creates human-made intelligence with faulty irrigation and inextinguishably thirsty. Our natural world, sunbaked, is destroyed, so the unnatural underworld, simulates symbols of the obsolete, mimicking the Queen of the Night†.
The heath† claims and empties us of sensualities–
pixelated petals of mayflower† do not fall, not ever.
Corruptible minds lose a desire for dripping pulp, their corporeality buried beneath the cabbage† bed.
Illusory heads of dogbane† are placed over eyes,
the dog rose† busies rigor palms and blocks lolled mouths.
Seeds, unsubmerged, grow roots that trap, tangle bloodied spider lilies† to prevent separation, of man and machine.
